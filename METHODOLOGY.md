# 🔬 Analytical Methodology & Classification Framework

To overcome inconsistencies in corporate segment reporting across different networks, this dashboard applies a standardized 7-category classification framework to map and track line-item revenues consistently over a 10-year timeline.

---

## 📐 7 Standardized Financial Categories

1. **TV Broadcasting > Terrestrial, BS, CS, etc.**:
   * Time advertising, Spot advertising, Program sales, BS/CS ad revenues.
2. **TV Broadcasting > Digital Ads**:
   * Streaming ad revenues (TVer, FOD, AVOD digital video ad streams).
3. **Content, Streaming, Production & IP**:
   * SVOD subscription content sales, Anime IP rights & global licensing, Movie box office & distribution, Production subsidiary revenues (SAND B, Studio Monowa, THE SEVEN, etc.).
4. **Merchandising & Lifestyle**:
   * E-commerce, direct mail order (Dinos, TV Tokyo Direct, Ropping), Retail (StylingLife Holdings / PLAZA, LightUp), Education (Yaruki Switch Group).
5. **Wellness**:
   * Fitness clubs, sports facilities.
6. **Real Estate & Others**:
   * Urban development, Akasaka/Odaiba real estate lease, Hotel & Resort operations (Granvista Hotels).
7. **Others**:
   * Miscellaneous segment adjustments and unallocated revenues.

---

## ⚖️ Consolidated vs. Standalone Adjustments

* Internal segment transactions and inter-company eliminations have been adjusted to prevent double counting.
* Consolidated total net sales and operating incomes strictly match reported Securities Reports (有価証券報告書).
