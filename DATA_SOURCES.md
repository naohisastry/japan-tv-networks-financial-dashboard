# 📚 Data Sources & References

This dashboard compiles and standardizes 12 years of financial statistics (FY2015 to FY2026) from official disclosure documents of Japan's 5 major commercial television networks.

---

## 🏢 Target Corporate Group List

1. **Nippon Television Holdings, Inc.** (TSE: 9404)
   * Core Subsidiaries: Nippon TV, Studio Ghibli, Tatsunoko Production, Madhouse, Hulu Japan, GYOKURO STUDIO
2. **TV Asahi Holdings Corporation** (TSE: 9409)
   * Core Subsidiaries & Joint Ventures: TV Asahi, TELASA (KDDI joint venture), ABEMA (CyberAgent joint venture), Ropping Life
3. **TBS HOLDINGS, INC.** (TSE: 9401)
   * Core Subsidiaries: TBS Television, SAND B, Studio Monowa, THE SEVEN, StylingLife Holdings (PLAZA, LightUp), Yaruki Switch Group
4. **TV TOKYO Holdings Corporation** (TSE: 9413)
   * Core Subsidiaries: TV Tokyo, TV Tokyo Direct, AT-X, TV Tokyo MediaNet
5. **Fuji Media Holdings, Inc.** (TSE: 9405)
   * Core Subsidiaries: Fuji Television, Dinos Corporation, Sankei Building, Granvista Hotels & Resorts, Pony Canyon
